One honest note

This is 30 minutes of guided meditation — not therapy, and not medical or mental health treatment. It's a small practice, not a substitute for professional care.

If you're already working with a doctor or therapist, keep doing that — this sits alongside it, not instead of it. You know your own mind best, so please take what's useful here and leave the rest, rather than reading too much into any one session.

If what you're carrying feels bigger than 30 minutes can hold, please reach out to a mental health professional or a crisis line in your area.
